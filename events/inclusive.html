GLOBAL STAGE Level: For everyone 🎤 Lecture

Inclusive by Design – creating applications for everyone

Did you know that sometimes one small detail in an app can make it... completely unusable for someone?

⏰
Time

12:05-12:50

📍
Location

TBD


Speaker

Agnieszka Hanna Gorlak

What is this event about?

We will look behind the scenes of app design, show how to create solutions that work for different people, and prove that accessibility doesn't have to be boring.

What will you learn?

  • You will learn how to design applications with different needs and ways of using technology in mind.
  • You will discover principles and solutions that help create more accessible interfaces — from simple decisions to specific UX/UI elements.
  • You will see what makes some apps intuitive for some and frustrating for others. Can you recognize when design truly works for everyone?

Who is this session for?

For anyone interested in interface design, user-friendly technology, and those who want to create better and more accessible software. No prior app design experience is required.
